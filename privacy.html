PBPB Dashboard

Privacy Policy

Last updated 15 October 2026

Who we are

PB Dashboard is an internal tool used by the Milele Motors marketing team to plan, edit, review and publish content for Fahad’s personal brand. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • Team accounts: names and work email addresses of team members who sign in, used only to show who did what on the board.
  • Content plans: video titles, schedules, captions and links the team adds to the board.
  • Microsoft SharePoint: the video files the team uploads to the company’s own SharePoint site.
  • Instagram and Facebook: when a team member connects the company’s Instagram account, the app reads the account name and posts reels the team has chosen to publish. It does not read private messages, followers or other people’s data.

How the data is used

Only to run the content workflow: scheduling, reviewing, publishing and recording that a post went live. Data is never sold, shared with advertisers or used for any other purpose.

Where it is stored

Board data is stored in Google Firebase. Videos stay in the company’s Microsoft SharePoint. Instagram and Facebook sign-in tokens stay in the browser of the person who connected the account and are not sent to the team’s shared data.

Deleting your data

To remove the app’s access to Instagram or Facebook, press Disconnect in PB Dashboard Settings, or remove the app under Facebook Settings → Business integrations. To have any other data deleted, contact the Milele Motors marketing team and it will be removed within 30 days.
